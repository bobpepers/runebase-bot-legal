# Runebase Bot: Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Runebase Discord bot ("the Bot") and its companion web app (such as [world.runebase.io](https://world.runebase.io)) collect, why, and what you can do about it. "We" and "us" means the Runebase Bot developer and operator.

If you have questions, contact us in the [official Runebase Discord server](https://discord.gg/CdUSaVfp8Q).

---

## 1. Information we collect

### 1.1 Discord account and server information

When you use the Bot in a server where it is active, we receive and store:

- your Discord user ID, username, display name and avatar
- the IDs and names of the servers and channels where you use the Bot
- membership events in those servers (joining, leaving, role changes, invites you create). These drive welcome messages, role rewards and invite rewards.
- the commands, buttons and menus you use, and their results

### 1.2 Messages

The Bot can read messages in servers where it is active, and messages you send it directly. It uses them to:

- **respond to commands** addressed to it
- **award chat activity points.** The Bot counts your messages. It keeps the text of your most recent message in temporary memory for **up to 1 hour**, only to detect repeated or spam messages, and then deletes it.
- **run spam control**
- **credit meme contributions.** When you post an attachment, we record the channel and message ID, but not the file itself.

**We do not store the text of your messages in our database.**

### 1.3 Moderation logs (set up by server admins)

If a server's admins turn on logging, the Bot posts records of server activity into a log channel inside that server. This includes edited and deleted messages, member joins and leaves, role and channel changes, invites, voice-channel activity and audit-log entries. These records live in Discord, under that server's control. We do not keep copies in our database.

### 1.4 Game and economy data

To run the games and the credits economy, we store:

- your game progress, such as characters, items, inventory, skills, quests, pets, buildings, clans, ranks, experience and leaderboard stats
- your credits balance and its history, including earnings, spending, gifts, trades and auctions
- game results, such as dice rolls, scratch cards, chests, chess and other minigames

### 1.5 Wallet and blockchain data

If you deposit or withdraw, or link a crypto address, we store:

- deposit addresses we assign to you
- withdrawal addresses you give us
- any public blockchain addresses you register, for example for holder roles or trading contests
- transaction IDs, amounts, fees, confirmations and timestamps

Blockchain transactions are **public by design**. Anyone can see them on the blockchain, and nobody, including us, can delete them.

### 1.6 Web app sign-in

When you sign in to the web app with Discord, we request the `identify` and `guilds` permissions. They give us your basic Discord profile and the list of servers you are in, which tells us which of your servers use the Bot. We do **not** request your email address.

- We store a session ID in a secure, HTTP-only cookie. The matching session, including the Discord access tokens needed to keep you signed in, is kept on our servers. A session expires after **7 days** without use, or after **30 days** at most.
- We use your IP address temporarily, for **up to 6 hours**, for rate limiting and abuse prevention.

### 1.7 Bot list votes

If you vote for the Bot on a bot list website, that site sends us your Discord user ID so we can give you the vote reward.

### 1.8 Technical logs

Our servers keep logs of timestamps, user IDs, commands and errors. We use them to fix bugs and keep the Bot secure. Logs are rotated and kept for a limited time.

### What we do not collect

We do not collect your email address, phone number, real name, payment card details or your Discord online status and activity. We do not read messages in servers where the Bot is not active.

## 2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

- run the Bot's features and the web app
- keep balances, items and game state accurate
- process deposits and withdrawals
- prevent fraud, cheating, spam and abuse, and enforce our [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md)
- fix bugs and improve the Bot

**We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.**

If you are in the EU or UK, our legal bases are:

- **performance of a contract:** providing the service you asked for
- **legitimate interests:** security, fraud and abuse prevention, and moderation features that server admins enable
- **legal obligation:** where the law requires us to keep records

---

## 3. Who can see your information

- **Other users.** Your username, game results, leaderboard positions, clan membership and announcements, such as rewards and level-ups, can be visible to others in the server.
- **Server admins.** They can see the moderation logs they set up (section 1.3).
- **Discord.** All Bot activity runs through Discord's platform, under [Discord's Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).
- **Blockchain networks.** Deposits and withdrawals on Runebase, Stellar or other supported networks are public.
- **Ecosystem services you choose to use.** Some features connect to other Runebase-ecosystem services, such as the RunesX exchange or Hermes. When you use those features, we share what is needed to complete your request.
- **Hosting providers.** They run our infrastructure and can only use the data to provide that service.
- **Legal requirements.** We may share information when the law requires it, or to protect the safety and rights of users or of the service.

---

## 4. How long we keep it

| Data | How long |
|---|---|
| Last message text (for spam checks) | Up to 1 hour |
| IP address (rate limiting) | Up to 6 hours |
| Web app sessions | 7 days of inactivity, 30 days maximum |
| Account, game and credits data | While you use the Bot, or until you ask us to delete it |
| Transaction records | As long as needed for fraud prevention, disputes and accounting, even after other data is deleted |
| Technical logs | A limited period, then rotated out |
| Blockchain transactions | Permanent (outside our control) |

---

## 5. Your rights

You can ask us to:

- **access** the data we hold about you
- **correct** inaccurate data
- **delete** your data
- **object to** or **restrict** some uses of it
- **receive a copy** of it in a portable format

To make a request, contact us in the [Runebase Discord server](https://discord.gg/CdUSaVfp8Q). We may ask you to confirm the request from the Discord account it concerns.

Deleting your data also removes your credits, items and game progress, and this cannot be undone. **Withdraw any withdrawable credits first.** We may keep transaction records where we need them (see section 4).

If you are in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

---

## 6. Security

We use encrypted session cookies, hashed credentials, access controls and other reasonable measures to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Protect your Discord account with two-factor authentication.

---

## 7. Children

The Bot is not meant for children under 13, or under the minimum age for Discord in your country. Features involving deposits, withdrawals or buying credits are only for users who are 18 or older, or the age of majority where they live. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

---

## 8. International transfers

Our servers and service providers may be in a different country from you. Where the law requires it, we take steps to keep your data protected when it is transferred.

We may update this policy as the Bot changes. We will update the effective date above, and we will announce significant changes in the Runebase Discord server.
